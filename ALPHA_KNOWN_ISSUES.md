# Known issues in the Curious Signals alpha

Thank you for testing. This alpha is meant to find problems, so expect some.
This page lists what we already know about, what you can do in the meantime,
and how to tell us about anything new.

## What has not been tested yet

- **The experiments haven't all been tried on a phone.** The seven Arduino
  experiments have new Setup and Calibration screens and extra exports, and the
  eight astronomy activities have new Setup screens and revised analysis. The 28 draft experiments
  are new. All of them pass our file checks, but some may not have been opened
  in phyphox on every phone and with every device before you receive them. If
  an experiment won't open, shows an empty screen or shows numbers that make no
  sense, please report it.
- **The draft experiments are drafts.** They appear in phyphox under the
  category "Curious Signals drafts", and their titles start with "Draft". Their
  set-up, calibration and interpretation haven't been checked with the real
  apparatus. Treat their results as something to check, not something to teach
  from yet.
- **The drafts need a recent phyphox.** If phyphox says an experiment needs a
  newer version, update the app from your app store. We don't yet know the
  oldest phyphox version that works.

## Things that behave differently from what you might expect

- **Calibration values start at nominal defaults.** The Calibration screen
  starts with textbook values, not values measured on your board. Changing a
  value recalculates the whole recording. Clearing the experiment data resets
  the values to the defaults, so note them down first.
- **phyphox may list other Bluetooth devices.** When phyphox asks you to pick
  a device for the Arduino experiments, choose the one named `phyphox-sense`.
- **Empty readings are normal for some channels.** The board sends a reading at
  most every 50 ms. Values a sensor can't provide show up as empty or "NaN"
  in exports.
- **Only the original Nano 33 BLE Sense is supported.** The "Rev2" board uses
  different sensors and won't work with this firmware.
- **The online preview shows example data, not a real board.** The preview
  page replays stored example traces so you can see the layout. It doesn't
  connect to anything and doesn't show what your board measures. It needs a
  browser from 2022 or later.
- **The example data and the research papers use generated data.** The
  example datasets in the repository, and the research papers that analyse
  them, use data generated by a program. They are not recordings from
  classes, so don't treat their numbers as real measurements.

## If the board doesn't appear in phyphox

1. Look at the small orange light on the board. If it **blinks three times,
   pauses and repeats**, the board's Bluetooth didn't start. Unplug it, plug it
   back in, and tell us if the pattern comes back.
2. Keep only one powered board near the phone. Every board uses the same name,
   so phyphox can't tell several boards apart.
3. Turn Bluetooth (and, on Android, Location) off and on again on the phone.

## How to report a problem

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page using the **Bug report** template. If
you can't use GitHub, send the same information to the person who gave you
the board. Please include:

- the experiment's name and which download it came from (for example
  "release v0.1.0-alpha.1");
- your phone model, its operating system version, and the phyphox version
  (shown on the app's information or About screen);
- the board or sensor you used (for example "Nano 33 BLE Sense", "SensorTag",
  "Puck.js", a Bluetooth mouse or a multimeter);
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot of the phyphox screen, and if you recorded data, the exported
  file (in phyphox: the menu, then "Export data", CSV format);
- for the Arduino board: what the orange light was doing, and if you can, the
  status lines the board prints. To see them, connect the board to a computer,
  open the Arduino IDE's Serial Monitor at 115200 baud, then connect from the
  phone. Copy the lines that start with `phyphox-sense:`.

Please don't post security problems in a public issue. Follow the
instructions in the project's security policy instead.
