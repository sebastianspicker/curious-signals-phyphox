## Scientific structure and merger decisions

The argument now begins with a visible-brightness integral and one detector-response kernel. A local-baseline estimator, its background assumptions and covariance are developed once. The four acquisition examples illustrate that common estimator under different reference conventions; the characterization sequence then separates amplitude, sampling phase and integration. Moon overlap and source-patch occultation are treated together as two different ways to recover light. The final two-luminous-body extension changes the denominator rather than borrowing the dark-planet radius formula.

Repeated apparatus descriptions have been replaced by one diagram with three configuration insets. Four compact topic figures use only existing prepared tables: detector-path comparison plus Nano; characterization features plus timestamp intervals; moon/spot brightening comparison; binary minima. The original complete traces and all source analyses remain available outside the active article. Four quantitative tables carry independent path comparisons, short-feature coverage, characteristic contrasts and binary depth/recurrence. This avoids concatenating the former sections and their introductions/conclusions.

The article explicitly treats the detector examples as independent runs. The SensorTag/phone/cell overplot uses each record's local time and first local baseline; it is not synchronization, a common-source calibration or an accuracy ranking. Solar-cell voltage remains a voltage deficit pending load-specific optical transfer. Device and receive clocks stay distinct. The diagram's common projection does not imply identical physical geometry across examples.

## Reference selection

Ten existing, verified references were retained: `transit2024` for the video-aided laboratory rationale; `exomoonlesson2023` for the source moon adaptation and its apparatus controls; `kipping2010` for temporal integration; `mandelagol` for the uniform-area special case versus brightness-weighted models; `apds9960` for optical gain/integration conditions; `hamadani2020` for load-sensitive low-irradiance photovoltaic characterization; `pont2006` for covariance in photometric inference; `sato2009` for the mutual-blocking brightening; `oshagh2013` for spot-induced parameter bias; `prsa2016` for the two-emitting-surface brightness integral.

The larger union of the source bibliographies was not carried forward. Shannon's theorem is not needed to establish the directly enumerated phase-coverage test. LUNA/Gordon-Agol details are outside this characteristic-area reduction; their omission does not imply that a full dynamical overlap calculation is unnecessary for orbital inference. Rackham's spectral-contamination treatment, Prša 2005 parameter-search methods and Torres 2010 absolute-dimension catalogue were omitted because this article neither retrieves spectra nor fits component masses and radii. Generic GUM/phyphox citations were not added. No new reference or new uncertain scientific claim required a bibliography change; the earlier per-source research records retain the broader searches and verified primary URLs.
