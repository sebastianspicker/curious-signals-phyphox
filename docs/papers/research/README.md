# Literature research for the expanded collection

The research records below cover all forty-three source investigations, now
developed in twenty-one topic papers. The [consolidation record](consolidation.md)
explains the topic boundaries, retained analyses and reference selection.
Each topic was examined
through its physical model, measurement response, calibration and interpretation,
with related experiments consulted where they add useful context. The records
identify sources read, claims they support, bibliographic checks and exclusions.
The articles retain the references needed for their argument rather than the
entire search result.

| Research record | Source topics covered |
| --- | --- |
| [Core sensing](core.md) | Specific force, angular velocity, magnetic field, barometric height, temperature/humidity, RGBC response and analogue voltage |
| [Reflectance and astronomical optics](astro_optical.md) | Albedo, transit photometry, Bluetooth multimeter acquisition and infrared temperature versus distance |
| [Thermal and planetary models](astro_thermal.md) | Enclosure heating, pressure differences in a model cabin, compression and star formation, and day/night thermal response |

The group BibTeX records preserve the additions and corrections made during this
revision. The authoritative merged bibliography is
[`references.bib`](../references.bib). A paper prints only the entries it cites.
The merge consolidates three shared records: Oshagh et al. (2013) uses the
DOI link, Renaudin et al. (2010) uses one consistently formatted record, and
SHT21 uses the directly checked October 2022, version 5 datasheet. These were
formatting and URL variants, with matching bibliographic identities.
Three duplicate publication keys are also consolidated: `zong2018photometry`
into `zong2018`, `pont2006red` into `pont2006`, and `luna2011` into
`kipping2011`. The topic records retain their original search labels; the
manuscripts use the consolidated keys.
The [earlier literature review](../literature-review.md) records the original
fifteen-paper search; the current topic records explain the expanded selection.

Reproducible numerical methods and results are recorded separately in
[`analysis/`](../analysis/), [`example-analysis.json`](../example-analysis.json),
[`draft-analysis.json`](../draft-analysis.json) and
[`revision-analysis.json`](../revision-analysis.json). Those calculations support
the worked examples; cited literature supplies the physical and metrological
context.
