# Observation-paper literature and analysis audit

Research date: 2026-10-26. Ownership: four new short papers and corresponding Markdown companions. All example inputs remain unchanged. Primary sources were searched from physical-model, measurement/uncertainty, experimental-analogy and hardware angles. Sources below are individually selected for a specific claim; abstract-only access is distinguished from full-text reading. No physical validation is asserted.

## pressure-boiling

Search angles/queries: “Wagner Pruss 2002 IAPWS formulation 1995 thermodynamic properties ordinary water substance DOI”; “IAPWS supplementary saturation properties ordinary water 1992”; “Blander Katz 1975 bubble nucleation liquids review”; “NIST water phase change pressure triple point”. The original lesson context was checked against `astro-lab_app/Material_SLab_ENG.pdf`, physical pp.15–17 and the prior archive audit; this establishes the syringe/phase-question motivation, not a simultaneous calibrated apparatus.

Selected:
- `iapws1992`, IAPWS, *Revised Supplementary Release on Saturation Properties of Ordinary Water Substance*, SR1-86(1992). Official [landing page](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/Supp-sat) and [release](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/Supp-sat.download) read; image-only PDF rendered and p.3 visually inspected. Verified Tc=647.096 K, pc=22.064 MPa and all six vapour-pressure coefficients/exponents. This is the numerical reference, inverted by bisection.
- `wagner2002`, W. Wagner and A. Pruß, *The IAPWS Formulation 1995 for the Thermodynamic Properties of Ordinary Water Substance for General and Scientific Use*, JPCRD31(2),387–535(2002), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1461829). Metadata and role verified through the [official IAPWS formulation overview](https://www.iapws.org/newform.html); AIP full text blocked. Used only for the established equation-of-state context; numerical coefficients come from the fully inspected release, not an unexamined equation in this article.
- `blander1975`, M. Blander and J.L. Katz, *Bubble Nucleation in Liquids*, AIChE J21(5),833–848(1975), [DOI/publisher](https://doi.org/10.1002/aic.690210502). Publisher metadata and abstract read. Supports the distinction between homogeneous/heterogeneous nucleation and a simple equilibrium onset, without importing an inaccessible detailed model.
- `nistwater`, [NIST Chemistry WebBook water phase-change compilation](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7732185&Mask=4), read table and reference links. Supports normal-boiling/triple-point context and the domain distinction.

Not selected: industrial IAPWS-IF97 adds an unnecessary second numerical framework; droplet-in-immiscible-liquid nucleation papers address different interfaces; generic pedagogical boiling demonstrations do not establish uncertainty or equilibration of the proposed vessel. No Mars-pressure extrapolation.

Analysis: all15 paired observations; only five code1 observations enter OLS ln(p/1kPa) against1/T. IAPWS saturation inversion at each actual pressure; residual u assumes entered0.1kPa and0.2K are independent standard uncertainties. Result42.5002kJ/mol is an apparent interval enthalpy, without an invented fit uncertainty. Boiling residual RMS0.2512K and maxconditional|z|2.1953. Code0/code2 mean residuals−4.9981/−0.9924K. Independent checks reproduce triple-point saturation and approximately101.418kPa at373.15K.

## spectral-fingerprints

Search angles/queries: “Seager Sasselov 2000 theoretical transmission spectra extrasolar giant planets DOI”; “Brown 2001 transmission spectra model extrasolar giant planet atmospheres”; “Charbonneau Brown Noyes Gilliland 2002 detection extrasolar planet atmosphere”; “Robertson quantifying resolving power astronomical spectra”; “NIST Atomic Spectra Database version5.12 sodium strong lines”. Original teaching context: `astro-lab_app/Material_SLab_ENG.pdf`, physical pp.6–12, Stations2–3. Calibrated, separately acquired spectra remain distinct from RGB broad-band measurements.

Selected:
- `seager2000`, S.Seager and D.D.Sasselov, *Theoretical Transmission Spectra during Extrasolar Giant Planet Transits*, ApJ537(2),916–921(2000), DOI10.1086/309088. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9912241), [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/9912241), and [CfA publication metadata](https://exoplanets.cfa.harvard.edu/publications/theoretical-transmission-spectra-during-extrasolar-giant-planet-transits) checked. Supports superposition/annulus context, not a claim the classroom ratio has transit geometry.
- `brown2001`, T.M.Brown, *Transmission Spectra as Diagnostics of Extrasolar Giant Planet Atmospheres*, ApJ553(2),1006–1026(2001), DOI10.1086/320950. [Preprint/metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0101307) and [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/0101307), particularly the spectrum-ratio and annulus discussion, read. Supports the diagnostic role and model dependence of spectral ratios.
- `charbonneau2002`, D.Charbonneau,T.M.Brown,R.W.Noyes,R.L.Gilliland, *Detection of an Extrasolar Planet Atmosphere*, ApJ568(1),377–384(2002), DOI10.1086/338770. [Author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544), abstract and bandpass/interpretation sections read; [institutional metadata](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/detection-of-an-extrasolar-planet-atmosphere/) verifies publication. Selected for the measured2.32±0.57×10^-4 scale and defined differential bandpass, not generalized instrument performance.
- `robertson2013`, J.G.Robertson, *Quantifying Resolving Power in Astronomical Spectra*, PASA30,e048(2013), DOI10.1017/pasa.2013.26. [Preprint PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.0871) introduction and resolution criteria read; [published PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/2CD1632ACF747D4872D2B3AD1FE18E67/S132335801300026Xa.pdf/div-class-title-quantifying-resolving-power-in-astronomical-spectra-div.pdf) verified exact final title/DOI. Used for LSF versus FWHM/resolving-power distinctions; not cited as a sampling theorem (the paper explicitly defers that topic).
- `nistasd2024`, A.Kramida,Yu.Ralchenko,J.Reader,NIST ASD Team, *NIST Atomic Spectra Database*,v5.12(2024), DOI10.18434/T4W30F. [Official history/citation](https://pml.nist.gov/PhysRefData/ASD/Html/verhist.shtml), [database page](https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-spectra-database) and [sodium strong-line table](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/sodiumtable2.htm) read. The latter gives air wavelengths5889.950/5895.924Å, separation0.5974nm.

Not selected: stellar colour-temperature relations do not solve the resolved-line problem; RGB-to-temperature calibrations would introduce a different observable; general spectroscopy encyclopedias add no primary evidence. Initial remembered DOI for Seager was explicitly corrected against primary metadata; Robertson's final DOI is10.1017/pasa.2013.26, not an assumed AS identifier.

Analysis: all21 common-grid bins,400–700nm,step15nm. Recalculate both dark-subtracted ratios and differential depth. At595nm local baselines are arithmetic averages of580/610nm; local depressions28.9445%/27.1005% and additional local excess0.0225193. No intensity uncertainties exist, so no invented significance or composition inference. Wavelength standard uncertainty0.2nm does not define resolution.

## coronagraph-contrast

Search angles/queries: “Lyot1939 study solar corona without eclipse DOI”; “Mawet2014 high contrast small sample statistics”; “Soummer Ferrari Aime Jolissaint2007 speckle noise coronagraphic images”; “EMVA1288 release4 linear2021 noise camera”. Original model context: `beitraege-forschung/unterrichtphysik-exo/513194-008.pdf`,physical pp.1–2 and corresponding DOCX Direct Imaging Experiment; this is a simple occulting-mask analogy.

Selected:
- `lyot1939`, B.Lyot, *The Study of the Solar Corona and Prominences without Eclipses*, MNRAS99(8),580–594(1939), DOI10.1093/mnras/99.8.580. [Publisher metadata](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/99/8/580/1085681) and [ADS original scan](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1939MNRAS..99..580L) read for historical optical suppression context. No detailed Lyot design equations are attributed to the cardboard mask.
- `soummer2007`, R.Soummer,A.Ferrari,C.Aime,L.Jolissaint, *Speckle Noise and Dynamic Range in Coronagraphic Images*, ApJ669(1),642–656(2007), DOI10.1086/520913. [Author preprint metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1739) and [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/0706.1739), especially sectionsIII.2/IV.1 on quasi-static components and off-axis companion response, read. The DOI was checked from the arXiv related-publication field rather than guessed.
- `mawet2014`, D.Mawet et al., *Fundamental Limitations of High Contrast Imaging Set by Small Sample Statistics*, ApJ792(2),97(2014), DOI10.1088/0004-637X/792/2/97. [Full publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/792/2/97), abstract, introduction and statistical discussion read; [author PDF](https://home.strw.leidenuniv.nl/~kenworthy/papers/2014ApJ...792...97M.pdf) confirms all12 authors. Supports caution about false-alarm interpretation and independent resolution elements.
- `emva2021`, European Machine Vision Association, *EMVA Standard1288*,Release4.0Linear(16June2021). [Official PDF](https://www.emva.org/wp-content/uploads/EMVA1288Linear_4.0Release.pdf) signal/noise model and linearity sections read; [release overview](https://www.emva.org/standards-technology/emva-1288/emva-standard-1288-downloads-2/) confirms the Linear/General distinction. Supports camera-response and noise requirements; no claim of EMVA conformity.

Not selected: generic NASA outreach repeats the astronomical motivation but does not add to the measurement model; reviews of sophisticated vortex coronagraph designs are not the physical setup here; software-specific ROI tutorials do not establish radiometry.

Analysis: all24 observations, two per mask/separation; six separations30–180pixels; exposure10ms. Group contrast is ratio of group-mean net signal to group-mean background. Plotted Q is mean individualN/uN, explicitly not a combined significance. Net/background/Q retention averaged across the six separations is0.36446/0.36054/0.78840. All six maskedQ means are lower. Conditional two-observation uncertainties use quadrature without assuming that covariance actually vanishes. Contrast uncertainty is left unidentified because required background covariance is absent.

Search angles/queries: “Larason Cromer sources errors UV radiation measurements2001”; “CIE2202016 characterization calibration UV radiometers”; “Larason Houston2008 spectroradiometric detector measurements”; “Nano33BLE Sense3.3V I/O analog inputs”. Original station context: `astro-lab_app/Material_SLab_ENG.pdf`,physical pp.13–14, Station4; quantitative external UV detection is an adaptation of the qualitative fluorescent/spectral station.

Selected:
- `larason2001`, T.C.Larason and C.L.Cromer, *Sources of Error in UV Radiation Measurements*, JResNIST106(4),649–656(2001), DOI10.6028/jres.106.030. [Original full PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/106/4/j64lar.pdf) signal integral and error sections read; [NIST publication metadata](https://www.nist.gov/publications/sources-error-uv-radiation-measurements) and [NIST issue contents](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/106/4/cnt106-4.htm) verify citation/DOI. Supports spectral/geometric/nonlinear errors and why an attenuation ratio remains response weighted.
- `cie220`, CIE, *Characterization and Calibration Methods of UV Radiometers*,CIE220:2016. [Official description](https://www.cie.co.at/publications/characterization-and-calibration-methods-uv-radiometers) read, including spectral quality indices and source-/detector-based calibration scope. Paid full report not accessed; no detailed numerical quality indices or unexamined clauses are asserted.
- `larason2008`, T.C.Larason and J.M.Houston, *Spectroradiometric Detector Measurements: Ultraviolet, Visible, and Near-Infrared Detectors for Spectral Power*,NIST SP250-41e2008, DOI10.6028/NIST.SP.250-41e2008. [Official full PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/SP/nistspecialpublication250-41e2008.pdf) calibration and uncertainty components, especially customer detector budgets and spatial response, read. [NIST record](https://www.nist.gov/publications/spectroradiometric-detector-measurements-ultraviolet-visible-and-near-infrared-0) verifies authors/date/identifier. Supports traceability and separation of calibration uncertainty from repeated-reading scatter; no transfer of NIST uncertainty figures to the example.
- `nano33` reuses existing verified Arduino bibliography key. [Official original-board documentation](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/nano-33-ble-sense/) and [datasheet](https://docs-content.arduino.cc/resources/datasheets/ABX00031-datasheet.pdf) establish the original board and3.3V I/O constraint; direct PDF fetch was403 on a later request, but official indexed text and prior verified source were available. Coefficients imply9.6774/7.7419/5.4839mW/m² for the first three; absolute uncertainty unspecified. Internal mismatch found and reported to parent: at identical device/receive timestamps, paired minus raw counts are[1,1,-1,-3,-1,1]. Inputs remain unchanged; manuscript discusses this as a sample-identity limitation, not a provenance note.
