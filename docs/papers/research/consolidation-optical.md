# Optical consolidation — October 2026

## Publication boundary and estimator decisions

Canonical `light-rgb` integrates `light-rgb`, `rgb-filter-transmission`, `puck-light` and `uv-transmission`. The argument follows one detector-response model through two distinct normalizations, reference bracketing, and the extra calibration needed for an absolute UV scale. The three instruments are not treated as simultaneous observations, interchangeable spectral responses, common specimens, or an accuracy ranking. The new title is *Optical Response, Reference Substitution and Detector-Weighted Transmission*.

The merged article introduces the response integral and dark/reference estimator once. It distinguishes RGB fractions (sum to one) from separately referenced transmissions (need not sum to one), rather than using “normalization” for both without qualification. Puck proportionality is explicitly a condition requiring a linearity test; LED detector literature establishes the mechanism, not this Puck's response curve. References are chosen for specific claims rather than retained as the union of four lists.

The merged article uses two new compact topic-figure sources (RGBC/filter response and LED/UV reference transfer), with separate physical clock labels. Retained articles reuse their original generated data figures. Every canonical paper has an apparatus schematic, a data figure and quantitative table. No analysis API or prepared-data transformation is introduced.

The albedo introduction was tightened and a redundant closing recap removed to prevent a bibliography-only third page; all equations, primary results and physical distinctions remain. The merged article uses a controlled column break before its references. Final LaTeX logs contain no overflow, undefined citations/references, missing glyphs or oversized floats.
