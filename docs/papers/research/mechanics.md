# Mechanics literature and analysis research

Research date: 2026-10-26. Primary sources were searched separately for each article; references were selected for the particular physical inference. Bibliography entries below are new; `geist2017` and `kok2016` reuse verified collection entries. Publisher access limits are stated rather than replaced by claims about unread sections. ## static-friction

Search angles and queries: (1) inclined-plane breakaway definition: `site.astm.org G219 static friction inclined plane 2024`; (2) contact history and slip onset: `Dieterich 1979 Modeling rock friction experimental results constitutive equations DOI`, `Rubinstein Cohen Fineberg 2004 detachment fronts onset frictional motion`; (3) smartphone teaching comparisons: `smartphone inclined plane static friction coefficient experiment accelerometer friction Physics Education`, `Baldock Johnson 2016 Investigation kinetic friction iPhone 065005`; (4) accelerometer geometry/calibration: NIST gravity-based three-axis characterization and mounting alignment.

Selected sources read and claims:

- `astmg2192024`: [ASTM G219-24 public scope/significance](https://store.astm.org/g0219-24.html). Verified title, designation and 2024 revision. Public scope supports the macroscopic breakaway/tangent definition. Full paid standard was not accessed; no conformance or detailed procedural claim is made.
- `dieterich1979`: [USGS-hosted original paper](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/static/lfs/research/rockphysics/Dieterich-1979-1.pdf), pp.2161--2163 and constitutive discussion. Verified author/year/journal/DOI against [publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1029/JB084iB05p02161). Contact age and loading history motivate controlling dwell and raising rate; rock-specific parameters are not transferred to the phone/ramp contact.
- `baldock2016`: [author institution record and supplied abstract](https://researchers.westernsydney.edu.au/en/publications/investigation-of-kinetic-friction-using-an-iphone/). Verified authors Clive Baldock/Roger Johnson, title, 2016, Physics Education51(6),065005, DOI. Publisher DOI did not resolve through the browser. Citation is confined to the abstract-supported distinction: dynamic acceleration can determine kinetic friction, whereas an onset angle estimates breakaway friction.
- `geist2017`: [NIST original article](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.122.032), abstract and calibration/alignment discussion. Verified existing metadata. Establishes the need to distinguish mounting geometry from intrinsic accelerometer response; our norm screen does not perform that calibration.

Considered but not cited: [Rubinstein, Cohen and Fineberg, Nature430,1005--1009(2004),10.1038/nature02830](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature02830), publisher abstract read; the interface-front imaging is physically valuable but unresolved in these data. [González Delgado et al., Papers in Physics10,100005(2018)](https://www.papersinphysics.org/papersinphysics/article/view/416), abstract read, provides an air-track analogue but adds little to the more specific kinetic-friction comparator. Susanti et al.2025 proximity-sensor study and university lab handouts were excluded because they concern dynamic trajectories or duplicate the inclined-plane definition.

Analysis: raw components determine inclination; gravity uses column3 as its time, linear acceleration column1. Arm5s, threshold1m/s², sustain0.15s, margin0.10s; previous gravity sample selected by timestamp. The endpoint angle is25.1043064deg and first confirmed excursion20.1420557s. Linear fits are diagnostic, not a claim of constant raising speed: the5--19.8s straight-line RMS residual is0.934deg, so local19--19.8s rate is used for a timing sensitivity. One trial cannot supply a trial-to-trial uncertainty.

## audio-magnetic

Search angles and queries: (1) relative audio amplitude and acoustic metrology: `smartphone sound level measurement calibration Kardous Shaw 2014 2016 external microphone`; (2) level conventions: `site.iec.ch 61672-1 2013 electroacoustics sound level meters frequency weighting`; (3) analogous motor-field monitoring: `stray flux acoustic motor monitoring external magnetic field motor sound primary study`; (4) vector calibration/background: `Kok Schon 2016 magnetometer calibration inertial sensors`; (5) block acquisition timing: official phyphox audio/sensor input documentation.

Selected sources read and claims:

- `kardous2016`: [NIOSH original author manuscript](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/43156/cdc_43156_DS1.pdf), methods and limitations; metadata verified at [CDC Stacks](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/43156). Calibration and microphone choice matter; their device-specific accuracy results are not assigned to this example.
- `iec61672`: [IEC official publication](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/5708), public scope and metadata. Verified edition2,2013-09-30. Frequency weighting/instrument response are part of sound-level metrology, absent from a digital RMS ratio. Full standard not accessed; no compliance claim.
- `liu2017stray`: [original IET article](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/iet-pel.2016.0304), sections2--3, especially3.1. Verified six authors,2017,10(2),189--195, DOI. Stray field can encode operating state; induction-machine/GMR calibration does not transfer to a phone beside a different motor.
- `kok2016`: [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.05257) and [full original preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.05257), section4. Verified published DOI10.1109/JSEN.2016.2569160 and16(14),5679--5689. Constant geometry and disturbance assumptions justify fixed-frame comparisons, not blind norm subtraction.
- `phyphoxinputs2026`: [official input reference](https://phyphox.org/docs/file-format/input/), audio/sensor sections. Actual recorded rate and delivered block lengths support energy weighting; sensor times must remain separate from audio processing times.

Considered but not cited: Kardous/Shaw2014 initial app survey, redundant beside the follow-up; [Adam et al.2011 PMSM stray-field study](https://journals.tubitak.gov.tr/elektrik/vol19/iss1/10/), less closely matched to the operating-state question than Liu; Gyftakis et al.2021 combined-current/stray-flux fault study, broader fault identification than the available observables; Mazaheri-Tehrani2022 overview, secondary literature rather than original evidence. Search results for acoustic motor classification were not used to infer a diagnostic capability.

Analysis: six8s plateaus, each stream selected on its own times. Compute energy-consistent aggregate RMS from N-weighted squared block RMS before logarithm; do not average dB as though it were energy. Three on-minus-preceding-off contrasts determine the quoted cycle spread.40 blocks lie at/below the0.010 noise amplitude and are stored as zero corrected amplitude; the physical inverse yields no positive resolved amplitude, so those are censored rather than interpreted as exact silence. Raw waveforms are unavailable for a new spectral reduction. No lag, phase, coherence or acoustic-to-magnetic transfer function is identifiable.

## magnetic-current

Search angles and queries: (1) current-to-field teaching arrangements: `measuring electric current smartphone magnetometer coil Physics Teacher`; (2) two-point implementation and geometry: official `Smartphones as ammeters`; (3) calibration regression/inversion: `site.itl.nist.gov calibration straight line inverse prediction`, sections2.3.6.1,.5,.7.2; (4) uncertainty in reference coordinates: `NIST errors in variables calibration Bartel1942`; (5) field offsets and rotation: Kok/Schön original model.

Selected sources read and claims:

- `phyphoxammeters2018`: [original experiment documentation](https://phyphox.org/wiki/index.php?title=Smartphones_as_ammeters), complete page. The downloadable configuration is dated07.11.2018. Supports two current-field pairs and explicitly warns that coil/phone movement changes calibration. No construction dimensions inferred.
- `nistlinear2026`: [instrument calibration models](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc361.htm), [model validation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc365.htm), [check-standard uncertainty](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc3672.htm). Full pages read. An intercept is retained and residual/check-standard information distinguished from absolute accuracy.
- `bartel2016`: [original NIST Technical Note1942](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/TechnicalNotes/NIST.TN.1942.pdf), introduction and errors-in-variables method. Verified author/year/title/DOI. Reference-coordinate uncertainty should enter calibration when appreciable; ordinary least squares against assigned ideal levels cannot quantify it.
- `kok2016`: section4 of the original preprint above. Fixed-frame scalar offset/gain are a local reduction of a broader vector response, not a general field calibration.

Considered but not cited: Wannous/Horváth2023 `Precise Measurements Using a Smartphone's Magnetometer---Measuring Magnetic Fields and Permeability`, DOI10.1119/5.0033597; title/authors/year/pages36--39 verified from the author institution annual report, but publisher text inaccessible, so no unread result is used. Kaps/Stallmach2022 solenoid teaching paper was found via ERIC, excluded pending primary full text. Generic Biot--Savart textbook content adds no necessary evidence beyond the explicit linear physical model and original current experiment.

Analysis: seven plateau windows1--7,9--15,17--23,25--31,33--39,41--47,49--55s. Known state assignments are0,.1,.2,.3,.4,.2,0A; they are not independent measured currents. The first/fifth plateaus define endpoint conversion; all first-five means provide a separate linearity diagnostic, and remaining plateaus test internal consistency. The exported current follows(Bz−30)/50 to rounding, so fitting that same channel against field would be circular. The mean-plateau residual, single-reading scatter and background-drift sensitivity are reported separately.
